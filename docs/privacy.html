Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Data collection

ApuSeq does not collect, track, store, or share your personal information. The local app settings are stored only on your device.

Files and Sequence Data

ApuSeq opens and edits files selected by the user. All file processing is performed locally on your device. ApuSeq does not upload sequence data or other user files to external servers.
